Welcome to Allahabad

About Allahabad

Allahabad, also known as Prayagraj,, is a city in the northern Indian state of Uttar Pradesh. It holds great historical, cultural, and religious significance in India. Located at the confluence of the Ganges, Yamuna, and the mythical Sarasvati rivers, it is a major pilgrimage center for Hindus. The city is known for hosting the Kumbh Mela, the largest religious gathering in the world.

Key Attractions

Some of the major attractions in Allahabad include:

  • Triveni Sangam - The confluence of three rivers and a major pilgrimage spot.
  • Allahabad Fort - Built by Emperor Akbar in 1583, this fort is a prime example of Mughal architecture. It is now taken over by army.
  • Anand Bhavan - The ancestral home of the Nehru family, which has now been turned into a museum.

Culture and Lifestyle

Allahabad has a rich cultural heritage, with a deep connection to Indian history and mythology. The city celebrates the Kumbh Mela every twelve years, which attracts millions of devotees. The locals are proud of their historical legacy and their contribution to Indian politics, literature, and education. Festivals like Diwali, Holi, and Eid are celebrated with great enthusiasm across the city.
